# T110 reference and design notes

Original local reference actually opened: `art/marca/boia-mascota.jpg`.
Also read `tools/blender/mascota.py`: orange buoy body, navy slanted cap,
large white eyes, cheerful smile and a white/navy float ring. The stage version
keeps those features with purple vest, gold sequins, pink bow tie, navy shorts
and shoes. This is a fully dressed, playful, non-explicit adult club act.

The silhouette is an original simplified geographic composition: twin gold
Intempo shafts with an open gap and inverted diamond crown, a stepped pale
Bali analogue and staggered narrow towers. It is not a surveyed reconstruction.
Palette and softened clay edges follow cala; geometry carries the screens'
equalizer bars and the oversized decorative cameras' lens/finder/shutter/tripod.

Club is open toward source -Y (glTF +Z), with a raised circular stage, two truss
columns, braces and an overhead reach supporting the pole. No roof conceals the
mascot. Screens and lamps have modest constant emission, no exported lights.
No camera APIs, texture access, video, external media or runtime behavior.
