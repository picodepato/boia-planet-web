## Concept in one paragraph

The Carnet stops being a profile page and becomes an object: a BOIA
membership card the size of a real ID card, printed in the wordmark's orange
with black ink, with the things an ID card has (a photo window, a document
number, fields with small labels, a QR, the machine-readable line along the
bottom) filled with BOIA's own facts (nickname, member number, rank, points,
member since). The security pattern is the sea: fine wave lines across the
orange. Turn it over and the back is a passport page on pale blue paper,
crossed by a nautical chart's rhumb lines, where each party leaves its rubber
stamp. The one bold thing is that orange card; everything around it (the
cream sheets of /mar, the night pages of the web) stays as it is. It still
communicates belonging, not status (REQ-IDE-012): no gold, no tiers of card
colour, the rank is one field among five.

## Card

- **Proportions.** ID-1: 85.6 × 54 mm, ratio 1.585 : 1, corner radius
  3.18 mm = 3.7 % of the width. The card is one block with
  `aspect-ratio: 85.6 / 54` and `container-type: inline-size`; every size
  inside is in `cqw` (1 % of the card's width), so it scales as a single
  object. Text sizes carry a floor (`max(9px, 2.2cqw)` and similar) so
  nothing goes under 9 px on a phone; the floor only bites below ~410 px of
  card width.
- **Sizes** (frame 06):

| Where | 375 | 768 | 1280 |
|---|---|---|---|
| /mar menu, «Mi Carnet» sheet (the sheet is full width − 20 px below 760 px, 390 px wide from 760) | 323 × 204 | 358 × 226 | 358 × 226 |
| `/carnet` and `/carnet/<id>` | 343 × 216 (full width − 32) | 560 × 353, centred | 600 × 378, left column; actions and answers in the right column |
| `/sello`, after stamping (back only) | 343 × 216 | 343 × 216 | 343 × 216 |
| «Descubrir a un BOIERO» in the ranking | 323 / 358 | 358 | 358 |

- **Shadow.** On cream: `0 10px 28px rgb(10 6 40 / .28), 0 2px 6px
  rgb(10 6 40 / .2)`. On the night page: deeper (`0 40px 80px rgb(0 0 0 /
  .55)`), the card is the lit object.
- **What stays below the card** (unchanged content, restyled as sections):
  the five answers (question small, answer large, REQ-IDE-015), badges and
  achievements, the ship and cosmetics, the own bottle, and, for the own
  Carnet only, «Tu cuenta». The card replaces today's `carnet-head` and
  `carnet-stats` (avatar, kicker, name, since, rank / points / stamps tiles)
  and the stamps section; it does not replace the answers.
- **Under the card, always:** the flip control (`↻ Ver sellos` / `↻ Ver
  anverso`) on the left and two dots (front / back) on the right. On the own
  Carnet, then: «Escanear sello» (primary, full width), «Editar mi Carnet»
  and «Compartir» (ghost, half width each). «Ver tus sellos» appears under
  the card when the back is showing and there is at least one stamp.
- **Guest** (frame 04, right): where the card goes, a dashed card-shaped slot
  with «Tu Carnet BOIA» and what it holds, then the invitation and «Crear mi
  Carnet», which opens the sign-in sheet with the reason `carnet`.
- **Local mode** (no Supabase env): the same card, filled from the browser's
  store; member number «—» (there is no server to number members), the local
  notice below the card as today. Nothing else changes.

## Back and stamps

Frames 02, 03.

- **The page.** Paper `#edf0f6` with a nautical chart's rhumb lines: 32 lines
  radiating from a point at (86, 56) cqw, in `#36278a` at 11 % (the four
  cardinal ones thicker), plus a small circle at the centre. Header: «Sellos»
  (Archivo 700, max(13px, 3.8cqw)) on the left, the count on the right
  («Ninguno todavía», «1 fiesta», «13 fiestas»; Inter 500).
- **Grid.** 3 × 2 cells from 11.5 cqw to the bottom; each stamp is 25 cqw,
  rotated by its own angle. One stamp per event, most recent first.
- **0 stamps.** Six dashed circular slots; the first one, darker, reads «Aquí
  va tu primer sello». Under the card the primary button is «Escanear sello».
- **1–6 stamps.** They fill the cells in order; the rest stay as dashed slots.
- **7 or more.** The five most recent and a sixth cell «+N / sellos más»
  (Archivo / Inter, ink outline). «Ver tus sellos» under the card opens the
  sheet «Tus sellos · 13» (frame 03): a grid of 132 px stamps, 2 columns
  below 768 px and 4 from 768, each with the event name (Inter 700 14 px) and
  date (`31 oct 2026`, Inter 400 13 px) in text under it. That sheet is also
  the readable, accessible version of the collection.
- **The stamp style — approved by Hernán (2026-10-03): the rubber-stamp
  look of these frames is THE stamp style** for every stamp, generated or
  from an image: one of the three shapes, one ink, the rubber texture, a
  slight rotation and this lettering. An inline SVG (viewBox 200 × 200) per
  event, with a rubber-stamp texture (an SVG filter: a 2.6 px displacement
  for wobbly edges and a low-frequency noise mask that knocks out a little
  ink):
  - *round*: double ring, the event name on the upper arc (Archivo 700),
    «ALICANTE · year» on the lower arc (Inter 700, tracked), the day·month in
    the centre (Archivo 700) and a ✺;
  - *rectangular*: double frame, name, a rule, the big day·month, «year ·
    ALICANTE»;
  - *oval*: an oval and a dotted inner oval, name, day·month, year.
  - Inks: red `#a32d12`, BOIA blue `#36278a`, ink `#1b1440`, orange
    `#c43a14` (all ≥ 4.9 : 1 on the paper). Rotation between −9° and +9°.
  - Shape, ink and angle come from a hash of the event id, so each party's
    stamp is always the same for everyone.
  - Uppercase lives only here, because that is what a rubber stamp says.
  - Without an image (or if the image fails to load), the event gets the
    generated stamp exactly as in frames 02, 03 and 09.
- **Image stamps** (Hernán, 2026-10-03; frame 17). An event's stamp can carry
  an image, uploaded as a file or attached from a URL in the Admin's
  «Fiestas y QR». **T94 builds the upload; T91 renders it.** The image is
  never pasted in the cell as a plain photo: it goes **inside the same
  rubber-stamp treatment**.
  - *Frame.* The event's shape (from the hash) keeps its frame and lettering,
    rearranged around an art window: round — the window is a circle of
    r 54 inside the inner ring, the name on the upper arc, «dd·mm·yyyy ·
    ALICANTE» on the lower arc; rectangular — a 132 × 86 window under the
    name, the date line at the bottom; oval — a 112 × 70 elliptical window
    between the name and the date.
  - *Fitting.* The image fills its window (`cover`, centred) and is clipped
    to the window's shape; it is never stretched. The Admin preview (T94)
    shows the crop.
  - *Ink.* The image becomes a one-colour print in the stamp's ink: its
    luminance becomes ink coverage (dark → ink, light → paper), quantised to
    a few levels so it reads as a rubber print, multiplied by its own alpha
    (transparent → paper). In the frames this is an SVG filter
    (`feColorMatrix` to coverage, `feComposite in` with the source alpha,
    `feComponentTransfer` discrete levels, `feFlood` with the ink). Then the
    same ink texture, the same 0.92 opacity and the same angle as a generated
    stamp.
  - *What works best.* High-contrast art: a logo, a drawing, a silhouette,
    on a light or transparent background. Photos work but read as dark
    blobs; T94's upload form says so in one line under the field.
  - *Limits recommended for T94.* PNG, WebP or JPEG (no SVG: an uploaded SVG
    can carry script; no GIF or animation); at least 512 × 512 px; at most
    2 MB uploaded. The server stores its own copy, re-encoded to WebP at
    512 × 512 (cover-cropped to the square), roughly ≤ 150 kB, which is what
    the Carnet loads. A URL is fetched once by the server with the same
    checks and stored the same way: the Carnet never hotlinks a third-party
    URL (privacy, and the image would vanish if the source changed).
  - *Where it shows.* Everywhere a stamp shows: the back of the card, «Tus
    sellos», the stamp received moment and `/sello`. Its accessible name is
    the same «{event}, {date}»; the readable name and date in «Tus sellos»
    are text, as for generated stamps.
  - *Stamp received.* The same drop as a generated stamp (scale 1.7 → 1,
    −18° → its angle, 220 ms, the 2 px knock). Reduced motion: a 150 ms fade.
  - *Loading.* The image is decoded before the drop starts (the claim's
    response carries its URL); if it is not ready in 1.5 s, or fails, the
    generated stamp drops instead.
- Stamps come from QR scans (decision 9) and, in local mode, from the test
  purchase as today (REQ-IDE-021). A stamp is a memory, not a transaction
  row (REQ-IDE-022): no amounts, no ticket numbers on it.

## Flip

Frame 07.

- **Trigger.** The «Ver sellos / Ver anverso» button under the card is the
  control; a tap or click anywhere on the card is a shortcut to it. The card
  itself is not a focusable button (it contains a QR and text; nesting
  interactive content in a button is wrong).
- **Motion.** `rotateY(0 → 180deg)`, 500 ms, `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1)`,
  perspective 1400 px on the wrapper, both faces with `backface-visibility:
  hidden`. Frame 07 shows 0 / 120 / 250 (edge-on) / 380 / 500 ms.
- **Reduced motion** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): a 150 ms crossfade
  between faces, no rotation.
- **Keyboard.** The flip button takes focus (2 px ink ring, 3 px offset on
  cream; white ring with a dark halo on the night page). Enter / Space flip.
  Focus stays on the button.
- **State.** The hidden face gets `inert` (and is skipped by screen readers).
  The dots say which face is up. Opening the sheet always shows the front,
  except right after a stamp lands, when it opens on the back.
- A polite live region announces «Reverso: 3 sellos» / «Anverso».

## Scan flow

Frames 08, 09, 10.

1. **«Escanear sello»** (own Carnet, under the card; also on `/carnet`).
   It opens the camera with or without an account: the QR is read first and
   the email is asked after, once a valid code has been read, so the code is
   never lost (Hernán, 2026-10-03; frame 10).
2. **Before the permission** (only while the camera permission is `prompt`):
   a sheet «Escanear sello»: why the camera is needed («Solo se usa mientras
   escaneas; no se guarda ni se envía ninguna imagen»), «Abrir la cámara»,
   and the alternative (the phone's own camera opens the link). Then the
   browser's prompt.
3. **Camera view.** A full-screen layer over everything (in /mar above the
   sea and the HUD; on `/carnet` above the page), rear camera
   (`facingMode: environment`), the feed covering the screen. A square window
   of 67 % of the short side (at most 320 px) centred a little above the
   middle, with four orange corner brackets (5 px, 44 px, 18 px radius);
   outside it a black veil at 58 %. Top: × (close) and, if the track supports
   it, a torch toggle. Above the window: «Apunta al QR de la fiesta» (Archivo
   22) and «Está en la entrada y en la barra». Bottom: «Cancelar». No shutter
   button: decoding runs continuously (`BarcodeDetector` or the on-demand
   decoder, T91).
4. **Read.** On a `/sello?e=…&c=…` URL: the corners close into a white frame
   around the window, the phone vibrates 40 ms if it can, the camera stops,
   the title becomes «Sello encontrado» with the event name and date, and
   «Guardando en tu Carnet…» shows while `claim_stamp` runs.
5. **Stamp received** (frame 09). The layer closes; the card is shown on its
   back; existing stamps shift one cell; the new stamp drops into the first
   cell: scale 1.7 → 1 and rotation −18° → its own angle in 220 ms
   (ease-in), opacity 0 → 1 over the first 180 ms with a 1.5 px blur that
   clears; at contact the card moves down 2 px and back (80 ms); a short dry
   stamp sound on the effects channel (respects Ajustes). Then a success
   notice under the card: «Sello de BOIA Halloween en tu Carnet. +50 puntos.»
   (also queued as a world notice for whoever closed the sheet). The next
   time the front shows, the points count up over 600 ms and a «Puntos 240 →
   290» chip sits in the control row until the sheet closes; a new rank also
   triggers «Ahora eres Timonel». Reduced motion: the stamp fades in in
   150 ms, no drop, no shake, no count-up. An event with an image stamp
   drops its image the same way («Back and stamps», frame 17).
6. **Errors** (frame 10), each a cream sheet over the paused, darkened
   camera, with the icon, a title that says what happened and one action:
   - outside the party's hours, before: «Este sello abre durante la fiesta»
     / «El QR de BOIA Halloween vale el sábado 31 de octubre, de 23:00 a
     07:00. Vuelve a escanearlo esa noche.» / «Entendido»; after: «Este sello
     ya cerró» / «…valía el 31 de octubre, de 23:00 a 07:00.»;
   - already stamped: «Ya tienes este sello» / «El sello de BOIA Halloween
     está en tu Carnet desde las 00:42. Hay uno por fiesta.» / «Ver tus
     sellos», «Volver a escanear»;
   - invalid (not a `/sello` URL, unknown or retired event, wrong code): «Este
     QR no es un sello de BOIA» / «Puede ser otro código del local o un QR
     antiguo. Busca el cartel con el QR de la fiesta, en la entrada o en la
     barra.» / «Volver a escanear»;
   - no account: the sign-in sheet, reason `stamp`, with the event named in
     the «why» line; after signing in the stamp is claimed with the code
     already read;
   - offline: «No hay conexión» / «Tu sello no se ha guardado: vuelve a
     escanear cuando tengas red.»;
   - permission denied (frame 08): a full-screen dark state «No podemos usar
     la cámara», how to enable it (site settings, the padlock by the
     address), «Volver a intentarlo», and the phone-camera alternative; the
     same screen with the title «Este dispositivo no tiene cámara» when there
     is none.

## /sello page

Frame 11. What opens when the phone's own camera reads the party's QR.

- A plain web page in the landing's language (night `#07101f`, the header
  with the orange wordmark, tracked labels, Archivo for the event name),
  not the 3D sea: it must load fast in a dark venue on a poor connection.
- On load it reads `e` and `c`, removes `c` from the address bar with
  `history.replaceState` (so a copied or shared link does not carry the
  code), and keeps it in memory for the claim.
- Header block: tracked label «Sello de la fiesta», the event name (h1,
  Archivo 28), date · hours · place.
- States: **Comprobando** (a spinner in a panel, «Comprobando el sello…»);
  **guest** (the sign-in panel inline with the reason `stamp`; on success the
  claim runs on its own); **stamped** (the card's back, 343 px, with the stamp
  landing as in frame 09, «Sellado. +50 puntos en tu Carnet.», «Ver mi
  Carnet» primary and «Zarpar al mar» ghost); **error** (the texts of the scan
  errors, with «Ir a BOIA.PLANET»).
- Local mode: the page says the stamp needs the online version and links to
  `/` (no claim is possible without the server).

## Sign-in sheet

Frames 12, 13. One sheet for every reason (decision 1). In /mar it is a cream
bottom sheet sized to its content (it never covers the HUD's top row); on the
web pages it is a night panel (`#0b1830`, hairline border) inline.

- **Header.** Kicker «‹ Volver» (closes and leaves everything as it was), the
  title by reason, a step bar (2 bars, 3 for a new account), ×.

| `skin` | Guarda tu compra | Lo que compras se guarda en tu cuenta: lo tendrás en cualquier dispositivo. |

- **Step 1 · email.** Label «Tu email», `type=email`, `autocomplete=email`,
  16 px text. Help: «Te mandamos un código de 6 cifras. Sin contraseñas.»
  «Enviarme el código» (primary). «Ahora no» (link). Footnote: «Tu email
  nunca es público: en BOIA te ven por tu apodo.» Validated on submit, not
  while typing: «Revisa el email: le falta algo después de la arroba (por
  ejemplo, .com).»
- **Step 2 · code.** «Te hemos enviado un código a {email}. Cambiar» (back to
  step 1 with the email kept). One input (`inputmode=numeric`,
  `autocomplete=one-time-code`, `maxlength=6`) drawn as six 44 × 58 boxes
  (Archivo 26); pasting the whole code works; the sixth digit submits.
  Help: «Al escribir la sexta cifra se comprueba solo. Mira también en spam.»
  Resend: «Reenviar el código en 0:42» (60 s cooldown, counting down), then a
  ghost button «Reenviar el código». Errors (boxes turn red, message below):
  wrong «Ese código no es correcto. Revisa el último correo de BOIA.»;
  expired «Ese código ha caducado. Te enviamos uno nuevo.» (sends one);
  too many «Demasiados intentos. Espera unos minutos y pide otro código.»
- **Step 3 · new account only** (title «Crea tu Carnet»). «Último paso: así
  te verán en BOIA.»
  - Nickname, prefilled with the guest nickname when there is one; checked
    400 ms after typing stops and again on submit: «✓ Libre» (green
    `#1f7a4d`), «Ese apodo ya lo tiene otra persona. Prueba con otro.»
    (case-insensitive), «Ese apodo no se puede usar. Prueba con otro.»
    (offensive filter; it never says which word). Help: «De 2 a 30
    caracteres. Es público; tu email no.»
  - Checkbox, required, unticked: «He leído y acepto la política de
    privacidad (muestra).» with the link to the privacy page.
  - Checkbox, optional, unticked, separate: «Quiero recibir noticias de BOIA
    por email: fiestas y entradas. Puedo darme de baja cuando quiera.»
  - «Crear mi cuenta», disabled until the nickname is free and privacy is
    ticked, with the reason under it («Falta aceptar la política de
    privacidad.» / «Elige un apodo libre.»).
- **Welcome** (frame 13, right). The sheet becomes Mi Carnet with the new
  card and a success box: «Ya eres miembro de BOIA, nº 0042. Hemos pasado a
  tu cuenta lo que tenías en este navegador: 120 puntos, 3 logros y tu

## Accessibility

- **Contrast.** Every text pair above is ≥ 4.5 : 1 (table in «Palette and
  type»). Text on the card is never translucent. Stamps' text is decorative
  repetition of the list; the readable version is «Tus sellos».
- **Card semantics.** `<section aria-label="Carnet BOIA de {nickname}">`;
  the front as a description list (Apodo, Nº de miembro, Rango, Puntos,
  Miembro desde); the QR image `alt="QR de tu Carnet público"`; the
  machine-readable line, the wave pattern, the rhumb lines and the mascot
  `aria-hidden`. The back is a list «Sellos, {n} fiestas» whose items are
  «{event}, {date}»; the «+N» cell reads «y {n} sellos más».
- **Flip.** A real button with `aria-controls` the card; the hidden face is
  `inert`; a polite live region announces the face. Reduced motion: fade.
- **Targets.** Every control ≥ 44 px (buttons 48, ranking rows 42 + the row
  gap — the whole row is the link, 323 px wide, so the 44 px rule is met with
  2 px of padding added in the build), the flip button 40 px tall plus 4 px of
  hit area.
- **Focus.** Visible everywhere: 2 px ink ring with 3 px offset on cream;
  white with a 2 px night halo on dark pages and over the camera.
- **Camera.** The layer is a modal dialog (`aria-modal`, focus on «Cancelar»,
  Esc closes, focus returns to «Escanear sello»). «Sello encontrado» and each
  error are announced (`role="status"` / `role="alert"`). Nothing depends on
  the camera: the phone-camera link path always works.
- **Sign-in.** Labels on every field; errors linked with
  `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`; the OTP is one labelled input, not
  six; the resend countdown is not announced every second (only «Ya puedes
  reenviar el código»); the disabled «Crear mi cuenta» says why in text.
- **Delete dialog.** `role="alertdialog"`, focus trapped, Esc cancels.
- **Rankings.** Tabs are a tablist; rows are links with «Ver el Carnet de
  {name}» (existing key); the «tú» row has `aria-current`; «Mostrar más»
  moves focus to the first new row.
- **Motion.** Only the flip, the stamp landing and the points count-up
  animate, all in answer to an action, all off under reduced motion.
- **Language.** All copy by key in `apps/web/lib/i18n/`, Spanish, sentence
  case.
